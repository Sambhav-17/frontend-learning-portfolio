One component, two good tools

Flexbox or
Grid?

Both can arrange a row of cards. The best choice depends on what the layout needs to control: one dimension, or two?

01

Flexbox

One dimension · content-led

01One row at a timeCards size to their content
02Flexible itemsShare leftover space
03Natural wrappingWrap when needed

Choose Flexbox when the items flow in one direction and their content should influence their size. It is a natural fit for navigation, action rows, and wrapping card lists.

display: flex;
flex-wrap: wrap;
gap: 1rem;
02

Grid

Two dimensions · layout-led

01Control both axesRows and columns together
02Shared tracksAlign across the layout
03Place with intentSpan columns or rows

Choose Grid when rows and columns need to line up or items occupy planned areas. It is a natural fit for dashboards, editorial pages, and galleries.

display: grid;
grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr);
gap: 1rem;
✳

A useful rule of thumb: Flexbox for a row or column of things; Grid for a layout that needs rows and columns to work together. Either can solve many real layouts — choose the one that makes the relationships clearest.
